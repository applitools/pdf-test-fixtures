000Working Notes for the Millbrook Community Archive
11101These notes describe how the Millbrook Community Archive is run from week to week. They are written for the volunteers who staff the reading room and process new material, and they assume nothing beyond care, patience and a willingness to write things down. The archive holds the papers of a small town: parish minutes, shop ledgers, school registers, photographs, and the correspondence of families who never expected anyone to read it. None of it is valuable in the auction sense, and all of it is irreplaceable.
24302The archive exists because the county record office is forty miles away and accepts only what it judges to be of regional significance. A great deal of what matters to Millbrook falls below that line. The founding committee decided in 1987 that the town would keep its own record, in its own building, catalogued by its own people, and that decision has held through three moves of premises and two floods. The collection now runs to roughly nine hundred boxes and grows by thirty or forty a year.
3751524Material arrives in three ways: by donation, by deposit and by rescue. A donation transfers ownership to the archive outright. A deposit leaves ownership with the depositor and lends us custody under written terms. A rescue is anything carried in from a skip, a house clearance or a closing business, and it is treated as a donation until somebody with a better claim appears, which in thirty years has happened twice, both times amicably and both times over a cup of tea.
4871525Every accession, however it arrives, is logged on the day it comes through the door. The log records the date, the source, a one-line description and the name of the volunteer who received it. It does not attempt to describe the contents in detail; that is the job of cataloguing, which happens later and often much later. What the log must do is make certain that nothing can be in the building without a record of how it got there, and of who to ask about it.
5991526Nothing is thrown away on arrival. Duplicates, blank forms and obvious rubbish are set aside in a marked box and reviewed at the quarterly committee meeting, where the decision to dispose is minuted.
